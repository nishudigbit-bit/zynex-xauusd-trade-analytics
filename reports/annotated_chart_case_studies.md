# Annotated XAUUSD Chart Case Studies

These screenshots were supplied by the portfolio owner and show TradingView charts for XAUUSD, primarily on short intraday timeframes. They are included as examples of the owner's chart-marking process, not as proof of realized profits.

## How to review each screenshot

For each chart, add a companion journal entry with the date, timeframe, session, setup thesis, actual entry and exit if a trade was taken, stop-loss, realized outcome, and whether the rules were followed. If the screenshot is a plan rather than a completed trade, label it **planned setup**.

## Evidence standards

- The `RR` value written on a chart is treated as a **planned/annotated risk-to-reward ratio**, not realized performance.
- A screenshot alone cannot confirm whether an order was filled, whether slippage occurred, or whether a trade closed at the marked target.
- Avoid selecting only winning setups. Include losing trades, invalidated ideas, and no-trade examples for a balanced portfolio.
- Match screenshots to trade records using timestamps where possible. Confirm the chart timezone before joining records.
- Do not infer P&L from chart movement alone.
